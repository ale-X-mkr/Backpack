Backpack is a private turn-by-turn navigation app for walking, cycling and driving, made for travellers and backpackers.

* Maps: OpenFreeMap / VersaTiles vector maps (OpenStreetMap data), day and night styles.
* Position: the phone's GPS chip only. No Google Play Services, no Google servers.
* Spoken instructions and screen in English, Spanish, French, Italian and German.
* Routing: openrouteservice with your own free key (an in-app guide gets it in 2-3 minutes), or your own server.
* Route options: avoid motorways, ferries, fords, steps; bike type and hills; walk or hike; prefer paved roads.
* Tolls: estimated toll cost weighed against the value of your time (Weekend / Work / Emergency).
* Speed ring with the road's limit and alarms (flashing red or blue with each alarm sound); optional fixed speed cameras (sound only, following each country's law).
* Well-known places along your route (Wikipedia / Wikidata), voice tips and readings, add them as a stop.
* Everyday services: fuel and charging, food, lodging, parking, toilets, water, pharmacy, emergency.
* Resume a stopped navigation, record your track, save trips as GPX.
* Places are fetched per ~5 km map square and kept on the phone: servers never see your route line.
* No account, no tracking, no ads.
