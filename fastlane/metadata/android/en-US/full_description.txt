Backpack is a private turn-by-turn navigation app for walking, cycling and driving.

* Maps: OpenFreeMap vector maps (OpenStreetMap data), day and night styles.
* Position: the phone's GPS chip only. No Google Play Services, no Google servers.
* Routing: openrouteservice (your own free key, or your own server).
* Route options: avoid motorways, ferries, fords, steps; bike type and hills; walk or hike; prefer paved roads.
* Tolls: estimated toll cost weighed against the value of your time (Weekend / Work / Emergency).
* Speed sign with the road's limit and alarms, loaded for the road ahead of your route.
* Well-known places along your route (Wikidata-ranked), add them as a stop or a short detour.
* Everyday places: fuel and charging, food, lodging, parking, toilets, emergency.
* Places are fetched per ~5 km map square and kept on the phone: servers never see your route line.
* No account, no tracking, no ads.
